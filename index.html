Common Questions

Start with the basics

These are some of the questions people often have when reviewing coverage.

What happens during a free protection review?

We discuss what you currently have, what you want to protect, and where you may have questions or gaps. The goal is to help you understand your options before you make any decision.

Do you only help with life insurance?

No. We can help with life insurance, health coverage, Medicare-related options, employee benefits, business protection, and other coverage needs depending on availability and eligibility.

Can you review coverage I already have?

Yes. You do not have to start over. A review can help you understand whether your existing coverage still fits your current needs.

Do you work with businesses as well as individuals?

Yes. We work with individuals, families, and employers looking for insurance and employee-benefit guidance.

Is My Premier Plans health insurance?

No. My Premier Plans is a healthcare membership program and is not health insurance. We can explain where a healthcare membership may fit alongside other coverage options.

How do I get started?

Call us at 956-630-1900 or request a free review online. We will start with a conversation about what you need help with.
