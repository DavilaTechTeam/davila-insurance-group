Referral Questions

Before you send someone our way.

Does the person I refer have to buy anything?

No. The purpose of the conversation is to help them understand available options and decide what, if anything, makes sense for their needs.

What if I do not know what type of insurance they need?

That is fine. You can still make the referral. We can begin by learning about their goals and the questions they are trying to answer.

Can I refer a family member or business owner?

Yes. Referrals can include individuals, families, and business owners who may benefit from insurance guidance.

Can they speak with someone in Spanish?

Yes. Davila Insurance Group offers assistance in English and Spanish.
