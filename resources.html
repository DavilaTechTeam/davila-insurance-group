Questions worth asking

What am I actually trying to protect?

Start with the financial consequences you want coverage to address: income loss, healthcare costs, debts, family responsibilities, business obligations, or retirement-related needs.

What would happen if I did nothing?

Understanding the financial impact of being underinsured or having the wrong type of coverage can help you decide what deserves attention first.

When was my coverage last reviewed?

If your family, income, business, health needs, or responsibilities have changed, your insurance may need to change too.
